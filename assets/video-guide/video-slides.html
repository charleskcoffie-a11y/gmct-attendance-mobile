GMCT Connect

How To Start Using The App

A short mobile guide for sign in, password change, contributions view, and adding the app to your home screen.

Important: Every member has a class number, and that class number is your ID for signing in. The church admin must add the account first.

Example username / ID: 121

First-time password: gmct2026

Follow the next slides step by step.

Step 1

Open The App And Sign In

Login screen
Example with member number and password
  1. Enter your member number or ID.
  2. Enter gmct2026 as your first password.
  3. Tap Sign In.
Step 2

Change Your Password

First login password change
  1. Type your new password.
  2. Type it again to confirm.
  3. Your new password must be at least 6 characters.
  4. Tap Update Password.
Step 3

View Your Member Page

Representative member portal screen

After sign in, this is where members can see their details. Some users may also see class leader options such as Mark Attendance, View Records, and Reports.

Step 4

See Your Contributions

This is where contributions appear

Tap View Contributions Dashboard from your member page to open this section and see your contribution summary and records.

Step 5

Add The App To Your Home Screen

iPhone

Open the app in Safari. Tap Share. Then tap Add to Home Screen.

Android

Open the app in Chrome. Tap the three dots. Then tap Add to Home Screen.

If you cannot sign in, please contact the church admin for help.

GMCT Connect is ready to use on your phone.
